# ABW-Projects-Garrett-Alstrin
This repository contains my assignments and exercises from Analytics for a Better World, a core course in the Business Analytics programme at the University of Amsterdam (Amsterdam Business School).
The course applies mathematical optimisation and data-driven thinking to real-world societal challenges, with a focus on making meaningful impact with limited resources. Work is connected to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and draws on tools from statistics, machine learning, and operations research.
